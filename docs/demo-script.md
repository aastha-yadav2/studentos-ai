# 3–5 minute demo script

## 0:00–0:20 — Hook

**Click:** open the auth page, hover **Explore the live demo**.  
**Say:** “Students do not have a motivation problem; they have a coordination problem. Coursework, applications, projects, and deadlines compete for the same limited attention.”

## 0:20–0:50 — Problem

**Click:** enter Demo Mode.  
**Say:** “Most planners produce a schedule once and leave students alone when life changes. StudentOS AI is built to learn from execution, not just intentions.”

## 0:50–1:20 — Solution

**Click:** point at the demo banner and dashboard metrics.  
**Say:** “This is a private, zero-setup workspace. The dashboard unifies productivity, consistency, goals, deadlines, and the next best action. Every insight is grounded in recorded work.”

## 1:20–3:10 — Live demo

**Click:** dismiss the walkthrough, scan the weekly chart, deadlines, recommendations, and goals.  
**Say:** “Aarav has a 12-day streak, strong evening focus, and career momentum on Fridays. StudentOS turns that evidence into recommendations instead of generic advice.”

**Click:** Planner; enter “Prepare for frontend interviews in six weeks”; click **Build orchestrated plan**.  
**Say:** “Before generation, the planner receives completed and skipped tasks, learned study habits, active deadlines, memory, and capacity. It streams its reasoning while it balances study, career, project, and productivity work.”

**Click:** show the completed roadmap, priority matrix, and AI reasoning.  
**Say:** “The plan makes trade-offs visible: it protects high-signal interview work, groups applications in the strongest window, and deliberately reduces lower-impact work.”

## 3:10–3:55 — Intelligence

**Click:** Reflection in the sidebar.  
**Say:** “Reflection closes the loop. Weekly reviews calculate completion, consistency, focus, and study time. Habit learning detects patterns; predictions flag risk; the AI Coach explains why each recommendation was generated.”

## 3:55–4:25 — Architecture

**Click:** optionally show README architecture diagram.  
**Say:** “React provides the experience. Supabase Auth, Postgres, and RLS protect each workspace. Edge Functions keep the OpenAI key server-side, and modular memory and reflection services make future vector memory straightforward.”

## 4:25–4:45 — Closing

**Say:** “StudentOS AI turns a student’s scattered commitments into an adaptive operating system—one that plans, learns, and explains. Demo Mode lets anyone experience that in seconds.”
